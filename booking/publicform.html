About STRA CARE

STRA CARE is a physiotherapy booking application designed to make it easier for patients to explore physiotherapy services, find clinic branches, request appointments and access convenient care options.

App Features

Easy Appointment Booking

Submit patient details and preferred appointment date and time.

Clinic Branches

Explore available STRA CARE locations and contact options.

Location Support

Use device location to attach your current location to a booking.

Online Payment

Access the available UPI and bank transfer payment details.

Booking Confirmation

Receive a booking reference and save your confirmation as an image.

Designed for Better Care

The app provides a simple digital connection between patients and STRA CARE's physiotherapy services, helping make booking and accessing clinic information more convenient.
