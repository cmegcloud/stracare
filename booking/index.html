Privacy Policy

STRA CARE Booking App

Last updated: 15 August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use STRA CARE to request an appointment, we may collect information such as your name, mobile number, selected branch, service, preferred date and time, and information you voluntarily provide for booking.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the booking form is used to process appointment requests, contact you about your appointment, coordinate with the selected branch, and provide physiotherapy services.

3. Appointment Information

Public visitors can submit a new appointment request. Appointment records are intended for STRA CARE staff and administrators and are not made publicly searchable through the application.

4. Branch Information

Branch names, contact numbers and location links may be displayed publicly so that visitors can select a convenient branch and contact it directly.

5. Data Security

We use Firebase services and access controls to help protect application data. Access to operational records is restricted according to staff and administrator permissions.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use services such as Firebase, Google Maps links, WhatsApp and telephone links. Their respective privacy practices may apply when you use those services.

7. Your Choices

If you need to correct or request information relating to an appointment you submitted, please contact STRA CARE through the relevant branch or official contact channel.

8. Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when the application, services or data practices change.
