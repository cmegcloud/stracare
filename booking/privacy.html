Privacy Policy

STRA CARE Physiotherapy Booking Application

Last Updated: 15 August 2026

1. Introduction

STRA CARE respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our application and appointment booking services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

Appointment Information

  • Patient name
  • Gender
  • Mobile number
  • Selected branch
  • Preferred appointment date and time
  • Service or treatment requested
  • Address or location information when provided
  • Additional notes submitted with the booking

Location Information

Current location may be requested when you choose to provide it, particularly when requesting home-care physiotherapy services. Location access is subject to your device permissions.

Payment Information

When an advance payment is requested, the application may record the payment amount, booking reference, UTR/transaction reference and payment acknowledgement details submitted for verification.

STRA CARE does not require you to provide your UPI PIN, card PIN, banking password or other confidential payment credentials.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and manage appointment requests
  • Check branch availability
  • Communicate regarding your booking
  • Verify advance payment acknowledgements
  • Confirm, reschedule or cancel appointments when necessary
  • Provide requested physiotherapy services
  • Maintain booking and operational records
  • Improve the application and booking experience

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Access to appointment and verification information within the STRA CARE system is restricted according to authorised roles and branch access.

5. Access by STRA CARE Staff

Appointment information may be accessible to authorised STRA CARE personnel for the purpose of managing and providing the requested service.

Branch-level access is intended to be limited to bookings associated with that branch, while authorised administrators may have broader access for operational and verification purposes.

6. Payments

The application may display a UPI QR generated for the applicable advance amount. Payments are completed through the user's selected UPI or banking application.

Payment verification may require the booking reference and transaction reference/UTR submitted by the customer. A booking request is not considered confirmed merely because a payment acknowledgement has been submitted.

7. WhatsApp Communication

If you choose to send a payment acknowledgement through WhatsApp, the information you enter and send will be handled through WhatsApp and according to its applicable policies.

STRA CARE may use the information received through that communication to identify and verify your booking.

8. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party technologies and services for hosting, authentication, database operations, payment-related processing, QR generation, analytics or communication.

Such services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Device Permissions

Certain features may request device permissions, including location access. You can control these permissions through your device settings.

If you do not provide optional permissions, you may still be able to use features that do not require those permissions.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for appointment management, payment verification, customer support, operational records, legal obligations and legitimate business purposes.

11. Your Choices

You may choose whether to provide optional information such as notes or location data. However, certain mandatory information is required to process an appointment request.

You may contact STRA CARE regarding questions about your personal information or a booking submitted through the application.

12. Children's Privacy

The application is intended to be used by patients, parents, guardians, caregivers and other persons arranging physiotherapy services. Where a booking concerns a child, the person submitting the information should have appropriate authority to provide it.

13. Changes to This Policy

STRA CARE may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, technology or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be made available through the application.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, appointment-related concerns or requests concerning information submitted through the STRA CARE application, please contact STRA CARE through the official contact details provided in the application or on the STRA CARE website.

15. Technology Partner

The STRA CARE application may be supported by technology and digital solutions provided by CM Filings.
