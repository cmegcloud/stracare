Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 28, 2026

At STRA CARE Physiotherapy, we are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our patients' personal, medical, and financial information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard the data handled through our management systems.

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality physiotherapy care and efficient clinic administration, we collect:

  • Personal Details: Name, age, mobile number, and address.
  • Medical Information: Patient history, physiotherapy assessments, clinical notes, and treatment progress.
  • Financial Data: Transaction records, payments, advance amounts, and outstanding balance details logged via our billing modules.
  • Location Data: Collected only when you opt-in to fetch your current location for home-visit mapping purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for:

  • Managing clinical appointments and staff schedules.
  • Maintaining accurate medical records to ensure continuity of care.
  • Processing payments, invoices, and managing billing/dues.
  • Communication regarding appointments and service updates.

3. Data Security and Sharing

We implement rigorous security measures to protect your information. STRA CARE does not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Information is only shared within our authorized staff and therapists on a need-to-know basis to facilitate your treatment and clinic operations.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal medical records, request corrections to your information, or ask for the deletion of your account data, subject to legal requirements regarding medical record keeping.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any significant changes will be notified to our patients through our clinic communication channels.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@stracares.in or visit our office at Stra Care, Head Branch, Kottayampoyil, Koothuparamba, Kannur District, Kerala State.
